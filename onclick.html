English Tenses Quiz

1. She ______ to school every day.

A. go
B. goes
C. is going

2. They ______ TV when I arrived.

A. watch
B. watched
C. were watching

A. finish

C. had finished
D. have finished

B. eats
C. ate
D. has eaten

5. By next week, they ______ the house.

B. will be painting
C. will have painted
D. paint

6. When I called her, she ______.

A. sleeps
B. is sleeping
C. was sleeping
D. slept

7. She ______ in this company since 2015.

A. works
B. worked
C. has worked
D. had worked

8. She ______ the movie three times.

A. sees
B. saw
C. has seen
D. had seen

9. While he ______ the dishes, the phone rang.

A. washed
B. has washed
C. was washing
D. washes

10. By 8 PM, I ______ for three hours.

A. will study
B. will be studying
C. will have studied
D. will have been studying
